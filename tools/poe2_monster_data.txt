1. PER-LEVEL TABLE (levels 1..100; 100 entries exist in every PoB table)
==========================================================================================
Columns from [PoB] Misc.lua: Life=monsterLifeTable, Damage=monsterDamageTable (rounded to 2 dp; raw values
are float32 e.g. 9.1599998474121), Evasion=monsterEvasionTable, Accuracy=monsterAccuracyTable,
Armour(PoB)=monsterArmourTable, AilmentThr=monsterAilmentThresholdTable, PoiseThr=monsterPoiseThresholdTable,
AllyLife=monsterAllyLifeTable, AllyDamage=monsterAllyDamageTable.
Armour(poe2db) and Experience come from [P2DB] /us/Monster table (columns there: Monster Level, Damage,
Evasion, Accuracy, Life, Armour, Experience, Minion Life, Minion Damage). poe2db's Damage/Evasion/Accuracy/
Life/Minion Life/Minion Damage match PoB exactly at every level checked (1,10,20,...,100).
!! Armour DIFFERS: poe2db per-level Armour column is higher than PoB monsterArmourTable (e.g. L80 6867 vs 4803).
   However poe2db's own boss pages compute armour from the PoB values (Xesht L80 armour 4,803 = PoB L80;
   Arbiter L74 4,099 = 3416*1.20). So PoB monsterArmourTable appears to be what the game/boss pages use;
   the poe2db per-level Armour column origin is unclear.
Levels > 83 exist in the data (table goes to 100). poe2db notes waystone area levels run 65 (Tier 1) to
80 (Tier 16); PoB caps enemy level at MaxEnemyLevel = 85, and pinnacle default level is 82.
Note the big life jumps at L70 (8918->11148), L75 (14887->18609), L80 (24852->31065): endgame step-ups.
Ailment threshold == life for L1-~30 then diverges; it also jumps at 70/75/80.
