Ice Shards (poe2db.tw/us/Ice_Shards) - crossbow ammunition. (Text paraphrased by WebFetch.)
Mechanic: Loads a clip of icy bolts fired rapidly at the ground; each bolt leaves an ice shard at impact that arms after a delay. Armed shard explodes when an enemy steps on it, dealing more damage the longer it has been armed (capped). Using again reloads.
Ammunition: +16 bolts per clip.
Projectile: 0 mana; 550% attack speed; attack dmg 8-29%; projectile speed 29.25 m/s; fires at ground, cannot chain; 60% phys->cold.
Shard: attack dmg 20-82%; 100% phys->cold; chills as though dealing 200-390% more damage (lv1-20); explosion radius 2.5 m; arms after 1.5 s; up to 400% more damage 2 s after arming; max 16 active shards.
Quality: chill as though 0-100% more damage; 0-200% more Freeze buildup.
Other: shard hazard damage reduced 80% for first 500 ms. Hidden: 8 m shard detection radius; 500 ms crossbow barrage debuff; 2 recoil per shot; 10 total recoil buff count.
