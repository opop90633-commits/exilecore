Flame Wall (poe2db.tw/us/Flame_Wall)
Type: Spell (Tier 1). Tags: Spell, AoE, Fire, Duration, Repeatable
Cast time: 1.00 sec | Base crit: 5.00% | Cooldown: none listed | Weapon req: none
Requirements L1-20: char level 1-90, Int 4-157. Mana 10-111 (L1-20).
Mechanic: Creates a short-lived wall of fire in front of you that deals Fire damage over time to enemies inside it and Ignites them. Projectiles fired through the wall by you or allies gain added Fire damage; if a Lightning Infusion is available it is consumed (Infused wall) and projectiles also gain added Lightning damage.
Stats (L1-20): wall deals 1.8-59.6 Fire damage per second; wall duration 4.5-6.4 s; Ignites as though dealing 5-8 to 181-271 Fire damage; wall length 5-6.9 m; projectiles through wall gain 2-3 to 27-41 added Fire damage; through Infused wall 1-4 to 3-65 added Lightning damage.
Hidden: active skill energy generated -50%; number of allowed firewalls 3; movement speed -70% while performing action (+160%/s acceleration, locked 50% duration); custom cast range 15.
Quality (0-20): 0-20% more Flammability Magnitude; 0-40% more Fire Damage.
Base Damage column: 100% x5 at every level.
