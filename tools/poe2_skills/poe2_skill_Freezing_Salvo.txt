Type: Attack (Tier 3). Tags: AoE, Projectile, Cold, Repeatable
Attack speed: NOT listed on page | Base crit: not listed (weapon) | Cooldown: none listed | Weapon req: Bows

Mechanic: Passively gains Seals over time (1 per 0.75 s, max 10).
