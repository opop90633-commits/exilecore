Shockchain Arrow (poe2db.tw/us/Shockchain_Arrow)
Type: Attack (Tier 11). Tags: AoE, Projectile, Lightning, Chaining, Payoff, Repeatable
Attack speed: 70% of base | Base crit: not listed (weapon) | Cooldown: none listed | Weapon req: Bows
Requirements L1-20: char level 1-90, Dex 4-157. Projectile speed 32.6 m/s.
Mechanic: Fires a lightning-charged arrow that homes toward enemies with Lightning Ailments. Hitting one releases a shockwave and chains to nearby targets (each chain hit also releases a shockwave); after the final chain the arrow lodges in the ground as a Lightning Rod. Consumes Shock (not Electrocution) from enemies hit.
Stats: Projectile converts 80% Physical to Lightning, cannot Shock; chains +3 additional times on first hitting a Shocked or Electrocuted enemy; arrow (rod) lasts 20 s and disappears after chaining 8 times; max 10 arrows active.
Shockwave: 2.5 m radius, attack damage 140%(L1)-507%(L20), converts 100% Physical to Lightning.
Quality: up to 20% more Lightning damage, up to 20% more attack speed (as summarised by fetch tool).
