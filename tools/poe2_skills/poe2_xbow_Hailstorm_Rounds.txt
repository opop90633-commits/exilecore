Hailstorm Rounds (poe2db.tw/us/Hailstorm_Rounds) - Cold crossbow ammunition. (Text paraphrased by WebFetch.)
Mechanic: Passively creates ice bolts at a rate tied to reload time, up to a storage cap. Activating loads all stored bolts which fire together and rain down on the target area.
Ammunition: 150% more reload speed; stores 32-50 bolts (lv1-20); quality +0-5 bolt storage, 0-60% cold res penetration.
Impact: 55% attack speed; attack dmg 63-228%; projectile speed 14 m/s; 80% phys->cold; 100-157% more Freeze Buildup; 100-157% more Chill Magnitude; fires +1 projectile; each enemy hit at most once per 0.2 s; impact radius 1 m; rain radius 4 m.
Hidden: 300 ms siege cascade fall time; 1000 ms queue override; 90 deg facing; -70% move speed, +160%/s accel, 50% locked duration.
