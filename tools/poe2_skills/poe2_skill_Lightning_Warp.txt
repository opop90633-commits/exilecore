Lightning Warp (poe2db.tw/us/Lightning_Warp)
Type: Spell (Tier 9). Tags: AoE, Lightning, Duration, Remnant, Repeatable
Cast time: 0.50 sec | Base crit: 11.00% | Cooldown: none listed | Weapon req: none
Requirements L1-20: char level 1-90, Int 4-157. Mana 8-83 (L1-20).
Mechanic: Target an enemy; if it is below the Cull threshold you teleport into it, destroying it and exploding for Lightning damage around you. If it is above the threshold it gets a 4 s debuff that triggers the same teleport/explosion if it falls below Cull during that time. Also works on Ball Lightning projectiles; targeting leaves Shocked Ground; success grants a Lightning Infusion; enemies that can be Culled are highlighted.
Stats: Explosion deals 2-30 (L1) to 54-1032 (L20) Lightning damage; explosion and Shocked Ground radius 3 m; Shocked Ground duration 8 s; debuff duration 4 s.
Quality (0-20): up to 10% increased Shock magnitude; up to 40% more Cast Speed.
Base Damage column: 100%,100%,100% at every level.
